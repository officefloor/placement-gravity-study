# Page-Jones, connascence (CACM 1992; *What Every Programmer Should Know About Object-Oriented Design*, Dorset House, 1995/1996)

**Not obtained, 22 November 2026.**

- CACM 35(9), 147–151 (1992), doi:10.1145/130994.131004. OpenAlex marks it open access at the ACM
  Digital Library, but the ACM site refused automated retrieval (HTTP 403 and a browser check)
  and no other copy was found.
- The book is not freely available. No freely available article or summary by Page-Jones himself
  was found; what is online are secondary summaries (blogs, talks, Wikipedia), which attribute
  three guidelines to him ("minimize overall connascence by breaking the system into encapsulated
  elements; minimize any remaining connascence that crosses encapsulation boundaries; maximize the
  connascence within encapsulation boundaries") and the "rule of locality" and "rule of degree"
  to Jim Weirich. These were not checked against the source and must not be quoted as his.
- Pescio (Ch. 12, November 2010) names connascence as the closest prior to his entanglement and
  dates the book "mid-90s"; that does not settle 1995 against 1996.
