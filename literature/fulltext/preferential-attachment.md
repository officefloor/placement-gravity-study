# Preferential attachment in software: Chaikalis and Chatzigeorgiou (2015, IEEE TSE); Lin and Whitehead (2015, MSR)

**Neither obtained, 22 November 2026.** Chaikalis and Chatzigeorgiou: no open copy (IEEE TSE);
an Academia.edu upload exists but needs an account and its status is unknown, so it was not used.
Lin and Whitehead: FLOSShub lists a PDF ("msr2015.pdf") but the link could not be resolved; no
other open copy found. Both remain as in §10 of 18 November (abstract and index records).

Read instead, as the preferential-attachment argument that bears most on the plan:
Taube-Schock, Walker and Witten 2011 (see its own note): preferential attachment weighted by
utility, plus limits on module size, gives scale-free coupling in 97 systems.

Pescio (Ch. 2, 2008, and his later Papers page) is the informal statement of the same idea
("large modules get larger (many have observed this power law distribution)"), under the name
gravity.
